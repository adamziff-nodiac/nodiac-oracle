# Data Center Industry Research Notes

## Market Overview (2025)

### Market Size & Growth
- **Global Colocation Market**: $69-105B (2024) → $165-295B by 2030-31
- **CAGR**: 22% projected growth
- **Vacancy Rates**: Historic lows at 1.6% in primary markets
- **Global Capacity**: 89 GW (2025) → 219 GW by 2030 (projected)

### Key Market Segments
| Segment | Size | Growth Driver |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Colocation | $163/kW/month avg | AI/ML workloads |
| Wholesale | $120-250/kW/month | Hyperscaler demand |
| Retail | <250kW leases | Enterprise cloud |

## Geographic Markets

### North America
- **Northern Virginia (Ashburn)**: World's largest data center market
  - Q2 2025 wholesale rates: $215/kW/month (record high)
  - Sub-1% vacancy
- **Dallas/Fort Worth**: Major secondary market, sub-1% vacancy
- **Atlanta**: Most affordable at $120/kW
- **Silicon Valley**: Most expensive at $250/kW

### Europe (FLAP-D)
- **Frankfurt**: 5.1% vacancy (lowest in Europe), DE-CIX hub with 18.1 Tbit/s peak
- **London**: $180-215/kW/month, largest European market
- **Amsterdam**: Strong connectivity but power-constrained
- **Paris & Dublin**: Growing markets with capacity constraints

### Asia-Pacific
- **Singapore**: $310-470/kW (most expensive), 2% vacancy, 60% of SE Asia supply
- **Tokyo**: $190-355/kW, declining slightly
- **Sydney**: $140-215/kW

## AI Infrastructure Demands

### Power Density Evolution
| Workload Type | Power per Rack |
|---------------|----------------|
| Traditional | 5-10 kW |
| AI Training | 30-80 kW |
| Next-gen AI | 100-150 kW (projected) |

### Key Trends
- 70% of future demand from AI workloads
- Liquid cooling adoption accelerating
- GPU availability constraining deployments
- Single AI training clusters requiring 50-100MW+

## Power & Grid Challenges

### Interconnection Crisis
- **Wait Times**: 4-7 years for grid connection
- **Transformer Shortage**: 30% shortfall, 2-3 year lead times
- **Substation Delays**: Approvals stretching to 2029
- **Power Costs**: $0.04/kWh (Quebec) to $0.18/kWh (California)

### Utility Constraints
- Large loads (50-500MW+) creating unprecedented challenges
- IRP planning cycles unable to keep pace with demand
- Transmission bottlenecks in primary markets

## Clean Energy Dynamics

### Corporate Procurement
- **PPA Prices**: $20-40/MWh for solar/wind
- **24/7 CFE**: Gold standard for sustainability claims
- **Curtailment**: 30-40% of renewable generation wasted in some markets

### Emerging Solutions
- Small Modular Reactors (SMRs): $10B+ in commitments
- Behind-the-meter generation
- Long-duration energy storage
- Hybrid renewable + storage projects

## Data Sovereignty Trends

### Market Growth
- Sovereign cloud: $97B (2024) → $649B by 2033
- CAGR: 23.8%

### Regional Requirements
- **EU**: Microsoft's three-tiered strategy, Google's "data shield"
- **China/India**: Strict data localization mandates
- **GDPR**: 2,245 fines totaling €5.65B through 2025
